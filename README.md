# Crazy Disc Golf

Turn every hole into a challenge round. Built by and for disc golfers who want more fun, more pressure, and more stories after the round.

**100% Free. Zero Ads. Zero Tracking.**

Crazy Disc Golf is a party-style scorekeeper and shot modifier generator for disc golfers. It adds controlled chaos to your round by rolling shot modifiers every throw, so each hole feels fresh, competitive, and unpredictable.

## Why Disc Golfers Love It

- **24 Built-In Disc Golf Modifiers** across 5 categories:
  - 🥏 **Disc Selection:** Driver Only, Mid-Range Only, Putter Only, Most Overstable, Most Understable, Your Choice
  - 📐 **Shot Shape & Line:** Pure Hyzer, Anhyzer / Turnover, Roller, Thumber / Tomahawk, Spike Hyzer / Grenade, Low Ceiling Skip
  - 🎯 **Grip & Stance:** Forehand (Sidearm), Backhand Only, Standstill Only, Patent Pending, Turbo Putt, Flamingo Stance, Non-Dominant Hand
  - 🔥 **Card Chaos (2+ Players):** Opponent's Choice, Bag Swap, Card's Call
  - 🍀 **Wildcard Bonuses:** Free Mulligan, Player B (Best of 2)
- **4 Preset Challenge Packs:**
  - 🌪️ *All-Out Chaos* — All 24 modifiers active
  - 🥏 *Casual League* — Balanced shot variety without blowing up your score
  - 📐 *Lines & Form* — Focused shot-shaping clinic for solo or field practice
  - 🎉 *Party & Trick Shots* — Rollers, Turbos, Off-Hand, and Bag Swaps
- **Custom Modifier Builder:** Add, edit, and toggle your own local course rules and inside jokes
- **Tactile Course UX:** Haptic slot-machine reel, tap-to-skip spin, live mid-round scorecard modal, Undo last throw, podium leaderboard, and 1-tap rematch with the same card

## Tech Stack

- Expo SDK 54 + React Native
- TypeScript + Expo Router
- Expo Haptics for tactile reel ticks and landing feedback
- AsyncStorage for 100% offline persistence (saved rounds, recent cardmates, custom modifiers)

## Run Locally

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm start
```
