Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05

100% Offline, Ad-Free & Private

Crazy Disc Golf is a free passion project built for the disc golf community. We do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data.

  • No Accounts or Sign-Up: You never need to create an account or share your email, phone number, or location.
  • No Ads or Trackers: The app contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • 100% On-Device Storage: Player names, round scores, and custom shot modifiers are stored exclusively on your device using local storage and can be cleared at any time inside the app or by uninstalling the app.
